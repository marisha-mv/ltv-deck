Our story

We bet everything on subscription.

The logic felt airtight: recurring revenue means rising lifetime value.
Win the member, keep the member, bank it every year.
We were half right — and the other half nearly cost us the business.
What we learned

Recurring revenue still leaks. You are not Netflix.

Customers are drowning in subscriptions. Yours is one cancel away.
Unless you're the one they can't live without, they eventually leave.
Recurring is necessary — but it is not the same as loyal.

If we couldn't hold them forever, we could give them somewhere to climb.

So we launched Mindvalley Academy — a ladder of premium programs above the membership.

The principle

Every rung buys more speed and more support.

Do it yourself
Courses & app — learn at your own pace.
Done with you
Coaching & cohorts — results in days, not years.
Done for you
Concierge, certification, identity, status.
Fast beats free. The quicker you get someone a result, the more they'll pay — so each rung sells speed, not just more content.

Now build yours

Design your climb in three questions.

1
The promise
Who is your customer trying to become? Sell them that — not the product.
2
The rungs
What's the next step that gets them there faster, with more of your help? Level 1 → 2 → 3.
3
The push
What win tells you they're ready for the next rung — and moves them up?
Every rung gives more speed and more support. Fast beats free.
